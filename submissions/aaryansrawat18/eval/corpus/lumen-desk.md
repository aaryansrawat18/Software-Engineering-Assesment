# Lumen Desk product note

Lumen Desk stores one attachment up to 25 megabytes.
Closed support tickets are kept for 24 months, then deleted.
The on-call rotation lasts one week and starts each Monday.
A yearly bonus is discretionary and set by the board. This note does not publish a formula.

Lumen Desk is the internal desk for customer notes. A note has a title, a body, and at most one attachment.
Search matches the title and the body. It does not search inside the attachment.
Deleting a note removes it from search after the next hourly index.
Editors can comment. Only the note owner can delete.
The status page lists open incidents. It does not list prices or a public roadmap.
A draft note is visible only to its owner until it is marked ready.
Ready notes appear in search within one hour. Comments do not change that clock.
Exports are a single text file. They leave out comments and the attachment.
The desk does not send email. Notifications stay inside the product.
