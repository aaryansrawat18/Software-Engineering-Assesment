# First week

On day one, new hires receive a 14-inch laptop and a badge.
The laptop is signed back in when employment ends.
The first 1:1 with the manager is on day three.
A teammate walks the new hire through the building on the morning of day one.

Day two is for accounts: email, the calendar, and the document drive.
The buddy sits nearby for the first five working days and answers where-to-find questions.
A short welcome note is posted in the team channel before the new hire arrives.
The photo for the badge is taken at the facilities desk, and the printed badge is ready the next morning.
By the end of the first week the new hire has shipped one small fix and read the product note.
The reading list is the product note, the hours note, and the leave note, in that order.
A laptop sleeve is in the same drawer as the charger. Both are returned with the machine.
The team channel is the place for questions that are not urgent. Urgent questions go to the manager directly.
Name pronunciation is written on the welcome note so the first standup uses it correctly.
