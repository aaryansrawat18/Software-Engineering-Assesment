# Field leave policy

Full-time staff receive 18 days of paid leave each calendar year.
Leave of more than 3 days in a row needs a manager's written approval.
Unused paid leave does not roll into the next year.
Sick leave is separate and is 8 days per year.
The office closes on the public holidays listed by the People team.
This note does not name a health insurer or a dental benefit.

Plan leave in the shared calendar at least one week before the first day away.
A request that overlaps a published launch week can be moved by the manager.
Half days count as half a day against the yearly balance.
The last day of paid leave in a year can be taken on the last working day of December.
Staff on leave keep their badge. They do not attend the standup.
A colleague covering the work is named on the request, and that cover lasts only for the approved dates.
