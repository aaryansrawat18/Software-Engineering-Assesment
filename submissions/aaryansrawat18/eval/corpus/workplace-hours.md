# Workplace hours

The core working window is 10:00 to 15:00 in the local office time zone.
Staff may start earlier or finish later as long as that window is covered.
Remote work outside the home country needs approval from the People team.
This note does not list salaries, bonuses, or equity.

The office kitchen closes at 16:00. Quiet hours on the main floor run from 12:00 to 13:00.
Headphones are enough notice that someone is concentrating. A tap on the shoulder still works.
Visitors wait in the lobby until their host walks down. The host is the person who sent the invite.
Desk lamps are switched off at the end of the day by whoever leaves the row last.
The building alarm is set by security at 19:00 on weekdays. Saturday entry uses the side door until noon.
Focus blocks on the calendar are respected the same way a meeting is. A block with no title still counts.
The printer on the second floor is for packets over ten pages. Short notes stay on the screen.
Mail is sorted at 11:00 and 16:30. Packages that need a signature wait at the front desk until the next morning.
